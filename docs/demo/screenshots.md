# Demo Screenshot Guide

Capture three small, readable screenshots during the walkthrough:

1. **Incident evidence** — incident header plus bounded Prometheus, Loki, Health, and Ticket evidence.
2. **Approval** — `WAITING_APPROVAL`, MEDIUM risk, proposed `restart_service`, and the approval control.
3. **Resolved timeline** — approval, fixed Ansible execution, verification, and final `RESOLVED` state.

Use synthetic demo IDs only. Crop browser chrome and never include `.env`, tokens, raw JSON-RPC,
terminal history containing credentials, or large log dumps. A GIF or video is not required.
